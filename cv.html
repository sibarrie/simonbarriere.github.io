Profil

Mechanical Fabrication Engineer with strong experience in precision manufacturing, subcontracting strategy, assembly supervision, supplier coordination and production follow-up for advanced accelerator technologies.

I currently work within the CERN Engineering Department, where I manage precision mechanical fabrication and assembly projects linked to major programs such as HL-LHC WP4 and LHC Long Shutdown 3. My work includes technical coordination, supervision of manufacturing activities, industrial follow-up and fabrication process development for superconducting RF cavities and HOM couplers.

My background combines mechanical engineering, innovation management and hands-on experience with high-precision machining, sheet metal forming, brazing, welding, cryogenic systems, cleanroom procedures and project management in international environments.
